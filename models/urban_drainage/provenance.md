## CA2D spatial reconstruction

The 3 m CA2D domain follows the interior of the registered orange modelling-region
boundary and is cross-checked against the 218 subcatchment polygons stored in
the baseline SWMM input. The user-provided modelling-region and road
screenshots are archived under `raw/references/` with their affine-registration
diagnostics. Orange road strokes are transformed to SWMM coordinates, expanded
to 8 m total width, and lowered by 0.10 m. Synthetic buildings of varied size
are placed inside suitable road-cut regions and raised by 15 m.

Ground elevation is an IDW interpolation of SWMM node cover elevations. For
junctions, cover elevation equals invert elevation plus maximum depth; for
outfalls, the invert elevation is used. This is a reproducible synthetic
benchmark surface and must not be represented as a surveyed DEM or building
inventory.
